Markets: setups boardRule-based setups with entry, stop and targets. Research and paper trading, not advice.

How to read this

  • Signal: the rule fired on the last completed daily candle. The backtest buys at the next session's open, so the entry shown (that candle's close) is an estimate; size from the actual fill.
  • Buy limit: a resting limit order for the next session only. Unfilled orders are re-evaluated the next day.
  • In the zone: price is inside the rule's buying zone but the entry condition has not happened. Not a signal.
  • Forming: would be a signal if today's candle closed at the latest price. Never act on an unfinished daily candle.
  • R = entry − stop. "Gap vs random" is the rule's backtest expectancy minus its random-entry control. A rule has to beat the control, not zero.

Risk rules

  • Risk 1% of the account per trade: shares = account × 1% ÷ (entry − stop).
  • At most 5 open positions, no more than 2 in one sector. Stops only move up.

Known limits

  • Prices come from the scheduled scan (every 30 minutes in the session), not a streaming feed, and are delayed.
  • Backtests use today's stock lists for all history (survivorship bias). 34 rules were tried; the best of many looks good partly by luck.
  • No earnings-date or sector filter is applied here.
